Privacy policy

Board

OpenJob collects nothing. There are no accounts, no tracking scripts, no cookies, and no analytics.

The board does not collect names, email addresses, or any personal data.

Job posts and talent profiles are public files in the GitHub repository, published by their authors under CC BY 4.0.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/softprobe/openjob.
