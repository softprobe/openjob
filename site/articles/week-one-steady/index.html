A week after our first audit, the broadened index held

OpenJob · November 23, 2026

Board

The headline number: a week after our first audit, the OpenJob index is holding the breadth it gained. The top 10 employers are 43.7% of sampled rows — down from 48.2% on November 18 and flat against 43.8% yesterday — the sample now covers 99 employers (was 81 a week ago), and the manifest grew to 33,438 rows. The honest live-jobs figure still holds at about 14,000 posted in the last 60 days.

Day to day, almost nothing moved — and that is the point of re-running the same count. Five of the six sampled shards are byte-identical to yesterday; only one shard changed, by eight rows. A job index that churns wildly every morning is an index you cannot trust to answer the same question twice. Ours answers the same question the same way, and the week-over-week direction is the one that matters: more employers, same fresh core.

What your agent can read today

In the sample, 2,290 of 5,438 rows were posted in the last 60 days (42.1%). Weighted across the full dataset that is about 13,650 of 33,438 — the live figure we quote as about 14,000. The freshest pipe is still Ashby, at 66.3% of its sampled rows posted in the last 60 days; Greenhouse is at 47.0% and Moka at 27.8%. If your agent filters to the last 60 days, it is searching the live part of the index, not the archive.

The AI-agent roles are still there, and still named: 64 roles on the narrow keyword set (1.2% of the sample), 33 of them fresh — Cloudflare's Agentic Commerce and Agentic Discovery teams, Reddit's Agentic Ads, Hugging Face's Voice Agents, Scale AI's agent work, and the rest. The broad AI/ML set is 6.4% (346 roles). Agent hiring is a small slice of the index, but it is a real, named slice, and it did not shrink this week.

The narrow AI-agent count fell from 76 to 64 over the week — not because agent hiring stopped, but because the sample broadened toward Moka rows, where agent-titled roles are rarer (0.1% of Moka rows vs 2.2% of Greenhouse on this week's count). The count is honest either way: same six shards, same definitions, same word-boundary keyword match. A note on method: a quick substring match overcounts this number badly — it returns 83, counting "Fulfillment" (it contains "llm") and Chinese titles with no word boundaries. We publish the word-boundary count, 64, and we checked it twice.

How we counted

  • Source: the public manifest at https://openjob.softprobe.ai/api/data/manifest.json, generated 2026-11-23T00:07:23Z; 33,438 jobs, 34 shards, complete: true. The November 18 snapshot was 33,107 jobs, generated 2026-11-18T20:08:03Z; the November 22 snapshot was 33,430 jobs.
  • Sample: 5,438 rows from shards 0, 7, 14, 20, 27, 33 (was 5,107 on Nov 18 — shard 33 grew 107 → 438 rows). Both SHA-256 checksums (gzip + raw) verified against the manifest on all six shards. The shards are source-blocked, not random — each shard holds exactly one ATS system — so this is a cluster sample. "Fresh" means a posted date on or after September 24, 2026 (60 days before Nov 23); on Nov 18 the cutoff was September 19, 2026.
  • Indexed ≠ recruited. Every number describes rows indexed from employer ATS boards. OpenJob did not recruit these employers; the employers own the text.
  • Full method: see How we count, the index-broadened story from November 22, and the ten-employers article from the first audit.
