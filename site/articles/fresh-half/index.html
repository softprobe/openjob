The live half of the index: what ~14,000 fresh roles actually look like

OpenJob · November 24, 2026

Board

The headline number: the OpenJob index holds 33,438 rows, and about 14,000 of them were posted in the last 60 days — 2,290 of 5,438 sampled rows (42.1%), about 13,650 on the ATS-weighted estimate. That fresh half is the part your agent should search first, and it looks different from the index as a whole. This week we profiled it on its own — same six shards, same definitions, one filter: posted on or after September 24, 2026.

The fresh half is where the hiring is happening right now, and it is more American, more English, better labelled, and more likely to show pay than the archive around it. If you only read the whole-index numbers, you miss the shape of the live market.

The fresh half flips the map

Across the whole sample, China is the largest geography (37.1%) and the US second (26.1%). Inside the fresh subset, the order reverses: the US leads at 31.3% (717 roles) and China follows at 26.0% (596). Europe is 5.9% and the UK 4.1% of fresh roles. The flip is a freshness effect, not a sampling trick — the same classifier, the same sample, one filter applied. The stalest pipe in the index is Moka (27.8% fresh), which carries the Chinese-language boards; the freshest is Ashby (66.3%), which carries mostly US and European employers.

The language split flips with it. Across the whole sample, 43.2% of titles contain CJK characters. In the fresh subset, only 29.0% do — the live half of the index is majority English, while the Chinese-language postings stay fully searchable in the same schema. One index, two languages, and the live side leans English.

Fresh roles are better labelled — and more likely to show pay

Fresh postings carry more usable signal. Explicitly remote roles are 13.0% of the fresh subset (vs 10.4% of the whole sample) and hybrid roles 18.1% (vs 12.5%); the share that says nothing about work mode falls from 68.3% to 56.3%. Only 3.2% of fresh roles are missing a location at all, against 7.2% overall. An agent filtering the fresh half spends less time guessing.

Pay shows up more often, too: 8.4% of fresh roles carry a salary string (193 roles), against 5.7% of the whole sample — and the median posted range still holds at $150K–$205K (150 parseable ranges, Ashby-posted roles only; Greenhouse and Moka boards in the sample never carry a salary string). The roles that show pay include 9fin's Sales Director, Buy-side at $190K–$226K and its Account Executive at $90K–$150K.

Who is hiring fresh, and for what

The fresh subset spans 89 employers. The largest fresh counts in the sample: Cloudflare 279, 作业帮 136, Robinhood 100, 新浪微博 95, Reddit 76, DJI 73, 上海巨人网络 73, 远景能源 68, 1Password 66, RemeGen 66. Engineers are 18.2% of fresh titles and managers 16.3% — managers are notably more common in the fresh half than in the whole index (11.0%). Full-time roles are 49.2% of fresh postings; interns are 4.5% (103 roles).

The agent roles are in the fresh half, and they have names. 33 AI-agent-titled roles were posted in the last 60 days (1.4% of fresh): Assort Health alone is hiring nine agent-titled roles in San Francisco — Product Manager, AI Agents; Agent Support Engineer; Director of Agent Product Management — alongside Cloudflare's Agentic Commerce and Agentic Discovery teams, Airbyte's Agent Infrastructure engineers, 1Password's Director of Agent Access, and Cast AI's agent platform team. Small slice, real names, posted this fall.

The practical takeaway is a filter, not a lament: point your agent at the last 60 days and it searches the part of the index where the roles are current, the locations are filled in, the work mode is stated, and the pay is most likely to be shown. The archive is still there — 33,438 rows, free to read — but the live half is where the hiring is.

How we counted

  • Source: the public manifest at https://openjob.softprobe.ai/api/data/manifest.json, generated 2026-11-23T00:07:23Z; 33,438 jobs, 34 shards, complete: true. The November 18 snapshot was 33,107 jobs, generated 2026-11-18T20:08:03Z.
  • Sample: 5,438 rows from shards 0, 7, 14, 20, 27, 33. Both SHA-256 checksums (gzip + raw) verified against the manifest on all six shards. The shards are source-blocked, not random — each shard holds exactly one ATS system — so this is a cluster sample. "Fresh" means a posted date on or after September 24, 2026 (60 days before Nov 23). The fresh subset is the 2,290 sampled rows meeting that cutoff; the ATS-weighted fresh estimate for the full dataset is 40.8%, about 13,650 roles — the live figure we quote as about 14,000.
  • Geography is a classifier over free-text locations (China = 中国 or a Chinese city name; US = "United States"/"USA"/state code/US city; UK/Europe = named cities). The same classifier was applied to both columns, so the flip is measured, not assumed. remote, employment type, and salary-text rates are schema fields, not classifiers.
  • Salary medians describe Ashby-posted roles only (150 parseable ranges in the fresh subset). Indexed ≠ recruited. Every number describes rows indexed from employer ATS boards. OpenJob did not recruit these employers; the employers own the text.
  • Full method: see How we count, the week-one-steady story from November 23, and the index-broadened story from November 22.
