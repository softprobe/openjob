The index broadened: three new employers joined the top 10 in a week

OpenJob · November 22, 2026

Board

The headline number: one week after our first audit, the OpenJob index got wider. The top 10 employers fell from 48.2% to 43.8% of sampled rows, the sample now covers 91 employers (was 81), and three new names joined the top 10 — RemeGen, 作业帮, and 千里科技. The manifest grew from 33,107 to 33,430 rows, and the honest live-jobs figure still holds at about 14,000 posted in the last 60 days.

A concentrated index is a narrow index. A week ago, half the rows came from ten employers; today it takes more employers to make half the index. That is the direction we want: more employers, more kinds of work, more reasons for an agent to search here.

Stripe, 阶跃星辰 (StepFun), and 赛智SAJ dropped out of the top 10 — not because they stopped hiring, but because the index added rows elsewhere faster. Envision fell from 312 to 129 sampled rows as its older postings aged out of the fresh window and the sample mix shifted. Concentration falling while the row count grows is the healthy pattern: the board is adding breadth, not just depth.

What else moved

Freshness is steady: 42.6% of sampled rows were posted in the last 60 days (was 44.0%), and the ATS-weighted estimate is 41.2% — about 13,760 of 33,430 rows, so the honest live-jobs number stays about 14,000. Freshness by source moved in both directions: Greenhouse got fresher (43.4% → 47.5%) while Moka got staler (33.4% → 28.5%); Ashby is still the freshest pipe at 66.8%.

The bilingual split deepened: 43.2% of sampled titles contain CJK characters (was 40.2%), and China is 37.1% of the sample by geography — but the freshest jobs still tilt American, with the US leading the fresh subset 30.8% to 26.5%. AI-agent-titled roles are 1.2% on the narrow keyword set (was 1.5%), 64 roles in the sample; the broad AI/ML set is 6.3%. Explicitly remote roles ticked up to 10.3% (was 9.7%).

Why this is a trend story, not a snapshot

One audit is a photograph; two is the start of a series. We re-ran the exact same method — the same six shard indices, the same definitions, both SHA-256 checksums verified on every shard — so the deltas above are comparable. The one thing that changed underneath us is that shard 33 grew from 107 to 430 rows (new Moka rows entered the index), which shifted the sample toward Moka. That is disclosed here rather than smoothed over: the broadening is real, and part of it is new Chinese-language rows arriving through the Moka pipe.

We will keep re-running this every week. If the top-10 share keeps falling, the board is doing what it is for: becoming the place where an agent searches many employers, not ten.

How we counted

  • Source: the public manifest at https://openjob.softprobe.ai/api/data/manifest.json, generated 2026-11-22T14:40:46Z; 33,430 jobs, 34 shards, complete: true. The November 18 snapshot was 33,107 jobs, generated 2026-11-18T20:08:03Z.
  • Sample: 5,430 rows from shards 0, 7, 14, 20, 27, 33 (was 5,107 on Nov 18 — shard 33 grew 107 → 430 rows). Both SHA-256 checksums (gzip + raw) verified against the manifest on all six shards. The shards are source-blocked, not random — each shard holds exactly one ATS system — so this is a cluster sample. "Fresh" means a posted date on or after September 23, 2026 (60 days before Nov 22); on Nov 18 the cutoff was September 19, 2026.
  • Indexed ≠ recruited. Every number describes rows indexed from employer ATS boards. OpenJob did not recruit these employers; the employers own the text.
