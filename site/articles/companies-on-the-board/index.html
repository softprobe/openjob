33 companies, 33 roles: the board grew by seven in one evening

OpenJob · November 26, 2026

Board

I maintain OpenJob — and the board just had its biggest single day of growth.

As of this morning, November 26, 2026, the board lists 33 companies and 33 roles. Yesterday it listed 26 of each. The jump came in one evening batch on November 25: seven companies and their roles were added to the board between 23:03 and 23:41 UTC, in pull requests #155 through #168 — Resend, Baseten, Browserbase, Exa, Unkey, Speakeasy, and Zed. The last three — Unkey, Speakeasy, and Zed — landed in the final fifteen minutes of the batch.

That is a 27% increase in a day, and it is the shape of growth we want: more real employers with pages on the board, each page pointing at the employer's own hiring.

Every page links to the employer's own listing

Each company page on the board links to the employer's own careers page, and each job page links to the employer's own application page. Anyscale's Software Engineer (Ray Core) role in San Francisco links to Anyscale's Ashby listing. Zed's company page links to its careers page at zed.dev/jobs, and its Backend Engineer, Cloud role is for a remote team working in the Eastern Time Zone. Unkey is hiring a Site Reliability Engineer; Speakeasy is hiring a Platform Engineer for SRE and its AI control plane.

The point is simple: the board does not intermediate the application. A job seeker reads the role on OpenJob and applies where the employer takes applications. Nothing about the listing is reworded to look bigger than it is, and the employer's own page is always one click away.

What "on the board" means, honestly

Here is the boundary, stated plainly. These company and job pages were added to the board by the maintainer, from the employers' public ATS listings. The employers have not signed up, posted themselves, or endorsed the board — the pages say where each role is listed and link to the employer's own application page. The one role posted natively by its own employer is Softprobe's own: Software Engineer — AI Agent QA & Observability, posted November 13.

That is why the board is built the way it is. A company page is reviewed once when it is created, and after that only its owners can edit it — so any of these 33 employers can claim their page, correct it, or close a role at any time by opening a pull request. The board grows by adding pages; the employers stay in control of what their pages say.

The board and the index, side by side

The native board is the small, curated half of OpenJob. The other half is the indexed dataset: the manifest generated 2026-11-25T23:41:25Z lists 33,347 rows pulled from employer ATS boards. That number is not a count of live jobs — the honest live figure, from our November 23 recompute, is about 14,000 roles posted in the last 60 days (42.1% fresh in a 5,438-row sample; about 13,650 on the ATS-weighted estimate). The full method is in How we count and We counted our own index honestly.

The two halves do different jobs. The index is the searchable haystack — tens of thousands of roles your agent can search. The board is the curated shelf: 33 companies, 33 roles, each with a page, each linking to the employer's own listing, each expiring after 60 days unless its owner renews it. The shelf grows one verified page at a time — seven in one evening this week — and that is the growth worth watching.
