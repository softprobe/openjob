Only 9.7% of jobs say they're remote; 68% don't say at all

OpenJob · November 20, 2026

Board

The caveat up front: every number below comes from our November 18, 2026 analysis of a 5,107-row sample of the OpenJob index (shards 0, 7, 14, 20, 27, 33; both SHA-256 checksums verified against the manifest). Percentages are sample percentages. The manifest listed 33,107 rows at analysis time and lists 33,362 rows today — but the row count is a cumulative index, not live jobs: only about 14,000 rows were posted in the last 60 days.

Of the 5,107 rows we sampled, 494 say they are remote — 9.7%. A further 671 say hybrid (13.1%) and 467 say onsite (9.1%). The remaining 3,475 rows — 68.0%, more than two in three — state no work mode at all.

The fresh subset — rows posted within the last 60 days — is a little more forthcoming: 10.2% remote, 17.9% hybrid, 12.8% onsite, and 59.0% still unspecified. Even among the freshest postings, most employers do not state a work mode.

The signal is missing in two places

The remote field is a schema enum (remote / onsite / hybrid / unspecified), and OpenJob's schema has it — most employers simply do not fill it. The free-text location field is no better: only 391 rows (7.7% of the sample) have location text containing "Remote", and "Hybrid" shows up as a location value 374 times — a work mode filed where a place should be. "Unspecified" does not mean the job is onsite; it means the listing does not say.

Why this matters for agents

An agent filtering for remote roles can only trust the 9.7% that say so explicitly. Everything else is a guess — and guessing wrong sends a candidate to a job they cannot take, or hides a flexible role from someone who could. That is the product point: the field exists in the schema, the data to fill it mostly does not, and the honest move is to publish the gap instead of imputing a mode the employer never stated.

How we counted

  • Source: the public manifest at https://openjob.softprobe.ai/api/data/manifest.json, generated 2026-11-18T20:08:03Z; 33,107 jobs, 34 shards, complete: true. Today's manifest lists 33,362 rows; the percentages here are from the November 18 snapshot.
  • Sample: 5,107 rows from shards 0, 7, 14, 20, 27, 33. The shards are source-blocked, not random — each shard holds exactly one ATS system — so this is a cluster sample. The sample has no Lever rows (Lever is 6.0% of the full dataset) and overweights Ashby. "Fresh" means the employer stated a posted date within 60 days of the snapshot.
  • Indexed ≠ recruited. Every number describes rows indexed from employer ATS boards. OpenJob did not recruit these employers; the employers own the text.
