Engineers are 1 in 5 postings; managers 1 in 8

OpenJob · November 20, 2026

Board

The caveat up front: every number below comes from our November 18, 2026 analysis of a 5,107-row sample of the OpenJob index (shards 0, 7, 14, 20, 27, 33; both SHA-256 checksums verified against the manifest). Percentages are sample percentages, counted by word-boundary match on the job title. The manifest listed 33,107 rows at analysis time and lists 33,362 rows today — but the row count is a cumulative index, not live jobs: only about 14,000 rows were posted in the last 60 days.

Roughly 1 in 5 postings in the sample is for an engineer, and roughly 1 in 8 is for a manager. Those two words dominate the index; everything else is a long tail.

Two things to note before reading the table as a ranking. First, the keywords overlap: a title can contain more than one of them — "Engineering Manager" counts under both engineer and manager — so the shares do not sum to 100%. Second, title counts can be inflated by a single employer repeating one title across many openings: one title in the sample appears 60 times, all from Chowbus (the story of that quirk).

What kind of work it is

Half the sample is full-time — 50.9% (2,599 rows) — and almost as much again does not state an employment type at all: 44.7% (2,283 rows). Internships are 3.0% (151), contractors 0.8% (41), part-time 0.3% and temporary 0.3%. As with work mode, the missing field is the story: nearly half the index leaves the basics unstated.

A department is present on 60.0% of rows. The largest are 研发部 (R&D department, 198 rows), Engineering (114), Sales (58), GTM (42), Operations (42), Marketing (36) and Technology (36) — the same engineering-heavy shape, in both languages.

What the index hires for — and what it doesn't

The index is an engineering-and-management board with a long tail of everything else. That is partly real — the employers in the index do hire mostly engineers — and partly a sampling fact: 81 employers supply the whole sample, and the ten largest supply nearly half of it. Broaden the employer base and the shape of the roles changes with it.

How we counted

  • Source: the public manifest at https://openjob.softprobe.ai/api/data/manifest.json, generated 2026-11-18T20:08:03Z; 33,107 jobs, 34 shards, complete: true. Today's manifest lists 33,362 rows; the percentages here are from the November 18 snapshot.
  • Sample: 5,107 rows from shards 0, 7, 14, 20, 27, 33. The shards are source-blocked, not random — each shard holds exactly one ATS system — so this is a cluster sample. The sample has no Lever rows (Lever is 6.0% of the full dataset) and overweights Ashby. Keyword counts use word-boundary matching on the title; role equals title on 100% of sample rows.
  • Indexed ≠ recruited. Every number describes rows indexed from employer ATS boards. OpenJob did not recruit these employers; the employers own the text.
