MongoDB is hiring a Site Reliability Engineer (Senior or Staff) for its Storage Layer Services team, the team re-architecting the MongoDB cloud storage layer at the heart of its next-generation cloud storage architecture. The team builds performant, multi-tenant distributed storage services that enhance today's Atlas storage stack and enable more customer workloads to run efficiently.

The role partners with the teams building these storage services to define SLOs, shape capacity plans, and ensure the reliability, durability, and operational safety of the storage layer that underpins Atlas. You will join a small, senior team of SREs as founding members of this organization, working on a multi-year roadmap for MongoDB's cloud storage architecture.

The role asks for 6+ years of experience developing software and operating distributed systems, proficiency in Python, Go, or a similar language, and experience operating stateful storage or database systems at scale. It also involves building for reliability across multi-cloud environments, participating in a 24/7 on-call rotation, and automating away toil. The role can be based in Boston, New York City, Raleigh, Miami, or Pittsburgh, or remotely in the United States in an Eastern or Central time zone. The listed US base salary range is $126,000–$248,000.
