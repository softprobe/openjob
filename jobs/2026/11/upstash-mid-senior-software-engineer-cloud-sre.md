Upstash is hiring a Mid/Senior Software Engineer for its Cloud/SRE team to run the production environment in Kubernetes and keep building the next generation of its serverless database platform.

The role improves the deployment lifecycle of databases and services, implements automation and deployment scripts as infrastructure as code, improves monitoring so problems are detected before users notice, and shares an on-call rotation for incident management.

The role asks for 3+ years of experience, a strong interest in distributed systems, Docker and Kubernetes in production, one of the well-known automation tools such as Terraform or Ansible, cloud platform experience especially with AWS or GCP, intermediate Golang and/or Python, and familiarity with Linux and its network layer. Contributions to open source are a plus. It is fully remote — work from anywhere in the world.
