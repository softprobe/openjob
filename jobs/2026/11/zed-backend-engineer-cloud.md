Zed is hiring a Backend Engineer, Cloud to help build the cloud services behind Zed and Delta. The server-side infrastructure powers many of Zed's core features: all AI connections go through this system, as do the collaboration primitives, in addition to cross-cutting concerns like auth, billing, and telemetry.

The team is rebuilding its backend from a single stateful server to a fully distributed system, and this role helps lead that effort. Projects include building Cloud Runners that provide ephemeral compute for development work, evolving billing, integrating with identity providers and authorization capabilities, and building integrations that connect Zed and Delta to developers' existing workflows.

The role asks for experience owning backend services from design through production, building and operating the cloud infrastructure behind your software, and leading projects other engineers contributed to while staying hands-on. Production Rust experience is a strong advantage but is not required. It is remote, in the Eastern Time Zone, across the Americas, and includes participating in on-call.
