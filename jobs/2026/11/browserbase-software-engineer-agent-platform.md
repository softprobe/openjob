Browserbase is hiring a Software Engineer (Agent Platform). Agent Platform is Browserbase's framework for automating browsers with code, and the team creates developer tools at the intersection of AI and open source, making browser automation smarter, simpler, and more powerful.

The role builds, operates, and grows the Agent Platform framework — developer-first APIs with about 2M monthly downloads and about 17k stars. The work includes designing and implementing tools that make automating the web simple and powerful, working with frontier AI labs to improve models and benchmark capabilities, and deploying production-ready AI solutions that impact millions of customers.

The role asks for strong coding skills, preferably TypeScript, experience building developer tools, frameworks, or libraries that others depend on, and experience building AI agents or LLMs, distributed systems, or applying reinforcement learning. Familiarity with web automation frameworks such as Puppeteer, Playwright, or Selenium is a plus. The team works 4 days a week in the San Francisco HQ, with Wednesday working from home. The listed base compensation is $180,000–$300,000 USD.
