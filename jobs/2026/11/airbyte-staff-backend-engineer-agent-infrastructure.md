Airbyte is hiring a Staff Backend Engineer, Agent Infrastructure. Airbyte is incubating new product opportunities at the intersection of data, AI, and developer infrastructure, and this small team builds tooling and infrastructure for data practitioners and software engineers on top of Airbyte's foundation in enterprise connectivity.

This is not an ML research or prompt engineering role. You will own technical explorations, build prototypes, and take promising ideas through to production: Airbyte MCP — infrastructure that lets AI agents configure Airbyte, troubleshoot syncs, and access data directly; semantic search and retrieval over enterprise data; semantic models; and a SaaS sandbox of realistic, stateful replicas of SaaS systems for developing and testing agents.

The role asks for 10+ years of software engineering experience building and operating production systems, strong backend fundamentals and distributed-systems design, and hands-on experience building and shipping AI-powered products using LLMs, agents, RAG, tool calling, MCP, or similar technologies. The role is onsite 4 days a week in San Francisco, CA. No salary is stated in the posting. The role was published November 11, 2026.
