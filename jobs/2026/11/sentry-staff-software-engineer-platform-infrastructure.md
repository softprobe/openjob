Sentry is hiring Staff Software Engineers for its Platform and Infrastructure Engineering teams, which build and operate the systems that power Sentry — cloud infrastructure, distributed storage, and real-time event processing — providing the technical foundation that enables the products and engineering organization to grow.

In this role, you will tackle some of Sentry's most challenging infrastructure and distributed systems problems: scaling real-time event processing systems, improving the reliability and efficiency of cloud infrastructure, and designing foundational services that support Sentry's products. You will own technically complex projects from identifying problems and defining solutions through implementation and production operations, and influence engineering decisions beyond your immediate team.

The role asks for 8+ years of software engineering experience, with a strong background in designing, building, and operating distributed systems or infrastructure, proficiency in at least one programming language such as Python, Go, or Java, and hands-on experience with cloud infrastructure and services such as AWS, GCP, or Azure. The role is based in San Francisco and hybrid. The base salary range for this position is $240,000–$400,000, plus an annual performance bonus of up to 15% of base salary.
