Anyscale is hiring a Software Engineer for the Ray Core team, which develops and maintains the Ray C++ backend — the distributed scheduler, language runtime, I/O, and memory subsystems — and is responsible for the reliability, scalability, and performance of Ray.

The work includes optimizing performance of large-scale workloads on Ray, stability and stress testing infrastructure, improving fault tolerance, and leading cross-team projects while mentoring junior team members.

The role asks for at least 5 years of relevant work experience, experience building scalable and fault-tolerant distributed systems, extensive experience in C/C++ and low-level operating systems, and a solid background in algorithms, data structures, and system design. Knowledge of distributed model training and inference and GPU programming is preferred.
