Speakeasy is hiring a Platform Engineer (SRE) - AI Control Plane. The platform enables enterprises and fast-moving startups to get the most out of Claude, Codex, Cursor, and other providers by securing agent sessions, leveraging enterprise identity systems to secure access to MCP servers, and giving teams observability into AI usage from token spend to complete agent sessions.

The role identifies architectural changes to improve reliability, performance, and availability, fosters a culture of reliability across the engineering organization, designs and implements operational processes such as deployments, upgrades, rollbacks, and postmortem review, and builds monitoring systems that ensure the highest quality service. It joins the on-call rotation and debugs production incidents across all services and levels of the stack.

The role asks for a record of improving a product's reliability and uptime, an exceptional ability to learn and work across the stack, and the ability to work in person in the San Francisco office, collaborating directly with the founders and customers on a daily basis.
