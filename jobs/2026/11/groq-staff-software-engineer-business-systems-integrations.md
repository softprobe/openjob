Groq is hiring a Staff Software Engineer for Business Systems & Integrations to build and integrate the enterprise systems behind its fast AI inference platform. The role sits in IT & Enterprise, connecting the business systems that keep the company running.

The role asks for a Staff-level engineer who can own business-systems integrations end to end, working across teams to design, build, and maintain the integrations between Groq's internal platforms. The person hired must be based in one of the three hiring hubs — the Dallas-Fort Worth Area, the San Francisco Bay Area, or the New York City Area — with some flexibility noted in the posting.

The listed total cash range, inclusive of potential bonus value, is $270,400–$318,100 for candidates located in the United States, with placement determined by geographic location, experience, skills, and internal compensation standards. Groq also offers a Long-Term Incentive Program and employee benefits.
