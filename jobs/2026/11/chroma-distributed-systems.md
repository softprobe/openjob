Chroma is hiring for Distributed Systems: scaling the retrieval systems behind its open-source data plane so it supports AI applications at scale — vector search, document storage, and metadata filtering for billions of records across millions of collections.

The role works on the distributed systems that make up the open-source Chroma data plane and power Chroma Cloud, alongside the Database Systems team and the Cloud Platform team, ensuring Chroma's distributed systems are reliable, scalable, and performant.

The role asks for experience building correct, performant, and reliable distributed systems at scale, familiarity with best practices in deploying and operating data platforms, and substantial production experience in Rust, C++, Zig, or other systems programming languages. A passion for testing through formal, lightweight formal, and probabilistic methods is a bonus.
