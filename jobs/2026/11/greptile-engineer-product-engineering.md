Greptile is hiring an Engineer for Product Engineering to build the core review agent and the product surfaces developers touch every day — the PR bot, dashboard, IDE and CLI integrations, and the shared components that keep them consistent.

The work includes improving the core agent — context retrieval, codebase indexing, memory, and comment quality — and automating the process of building Greptile, via Greptile, while building closely with customers both internally and within enterprises.

The role asks for a B.S. in Computer Science or equivalent, 1+ years of software engineering experience (5+ for senior or staff), demonstrated taste in technical and product decision making, and experience building agent products and interfaces is a plus. The team works in person every day in San Francisco, with founder-level ownership.
