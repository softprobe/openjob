Dust is hiring a Software Engineer to frame and build high-value features and pieces of infrastructure for its platform, where humans and agents work together to rewire how work gets done.

The role owns critical features end to end, scopes and leads technical projects to completion, collaborates closely with users to iterate rapidly, and designs for scale. The stack includes TypeScript, React, Node.js, Rust, Kubernetes, Terraform, PostgreSQL, Qdrant, and Elasticsearch.

The role asks for engineers who are dependable, move with purpose, and expand their impact as they learn the product — Dust hires every engineer as an Engineer regardless of seniority, and values potential over checking every box. The team is office-first, with in-person offices in Paris, London, San Francisco, and New York, and offers relocation support. The listed compensation band is €30,000–€150,000 base salary.
