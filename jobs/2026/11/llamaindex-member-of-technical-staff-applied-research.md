LlamaIndex is hiring an AI Research Engineer (Member of Technical Staff, Applied Research) to join its document understanding team and help define the narrative around document understanding.

The role sits between applied research and strong engineering: developing and training vision-language models for document processing and document understanding, building data pipelines for curation, synthetic data generation, labeling, and benchmark creation, and evaluating base models with post-training or fine-tuning to hit production targets for accuracy, latency, and cost.

The role asks for 3–7 years of experience in machine learning engineering, applied research, or research engineering, strong Python skills and comfort with modern ML tooling (especially PyTorch), and experience with computer vision, vision-language models, NLP, document AI, OCR, extraction, or agentic AI systems. The role works closely with the CTO and the document understanding team.
