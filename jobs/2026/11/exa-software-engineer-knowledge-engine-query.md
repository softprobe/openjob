Exa is hiring a Software Engineer, Knowledge Engine - Query. The Knowledge Platform team builds Exa's knowledge engine, continuously synthesizing information from across the web into one of the world's largest open-domain knowledge graphs, with hundreds of billions of evidence-backed claims.

In this role, you will own the query engine that searches hundreds of billions of claims across retrieval backends, from a distributed graph database to vector, full-text, time-series, and geospatial indexes. The work covers storage layout and indexing across engines, cost-based planning that picks the right backends for each question, and serving thousands of queries per second with measured latency wins.

The role asks for deep experience inside a distributed database, query engine, search engine, or storage system, a strong grasp of indexing, query planning and optimization, and storage formats, solid distributed systems fundamentals, and strength in a systems language such as Go, Rust, or C++. This is an in-person opportunity in Zurich, and Exa sponsors visas for international candidates.
