Asymptote Labs is hiring a Founding Developer Relations Lead to build the defining open-source community in AI agent security. The role grows and nurtures the Beacon open-source community, contributes code, examples, integrations, and tooling, and becomes one of the leading voices in AI agent security, working directly with Asymptote's founders.

This is not a traditional DevRel role: the hire helps drive adoption of Beacon, shapes developer strategy, influences product direction, and helps establish Beacon as the open standard for AI agent telemetry, with exceptional autonomy and ownership from day one. It is based in New York City.

The role asks for 5+ years in Developer Relations, Developer Advocacy, Solutions Engineering, or a similarly technical go-to-market role, experience at a high-growth AI, developer tools, infrastructure, or cybersecurity company, a strong software engineering background, and a proven track record of creating technical content that drives developer adoption. The listed compensation is a $150,000–$300,000 base plus meaningful founding-stage equity.
