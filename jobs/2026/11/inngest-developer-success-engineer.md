Inngest is hiring a Developer Success Engineer — a unique combination of hands-on engineering, problem-solving, and human connection. As the bridge between the product and the people who use it, the role works directly with developers: helping plan their architecture, solving problems, debugging issues, and offering guidance.

The role delivers expert-level support from simple troubleshooting to in-depth architectural guidance, tracks down and documents bugs with the engineering team, identifies feature requests, contributes to internal and public-facing documentation, and supports sales-led proof-of-concepts.

The role asks for expertise in TypeScript development with openness to other languages such as Python or Golang, knowledge of queueing systems, outstanding written and verbal communication, and a track record of helping other developers succeed. It is based in or near San Francisco, collaborating in person a few days a week, with unlimited PTO, 100% covered health insurance premiums, and company equity.
