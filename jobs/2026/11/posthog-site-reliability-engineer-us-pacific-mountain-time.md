PostHog is hiring a Site Reliability Engineer (US - Pacific/mountain time) for its Cloud Foundations team. This is not a typical "keep the lights on" SRE role: the work is about turning a fast-growing, stateful system into a predictable, well-automated platform — provisioning, scaling, rebalancing, and recovery — reducing operational stress and building the tooling and patterns that let the system scale without scaling human effort.

You will operate EKS clusters across several environments with Karpenter autoscaling, Cilium networking, and ArgoCD-driven GitOps deployments; manage and evolve a multi AWS account organization; maintain the Terraform/Terragrunt infrastructure-as-code platform; improve operational tooling around deploys, schema changes, backups, restores, and incident response; and optimize cloud spend as you go. The platform runs at large scale — petabytes of data, thousands of cores, constant ingestion — across a multi-region, multi-account AWS platform running many services on Kubernetes.

The role is for people in the US/Canada, GMT -8 to GMT -6, who like deep ownership of production systems and are not afraid of stateful infrastructure, AWS, VMs, and automation. It asks for deep hands-on experience with Kubernetes in production (EKS preferred), strong experience operating production infrastructure on AWS, experience hardening production infrastructure, and experience automating infrastructure with Terraform or Terragrunt at scale. PostHog publishes its pay openly through a salary calculator that varies by location, so no fixed range is stated in the posting.
