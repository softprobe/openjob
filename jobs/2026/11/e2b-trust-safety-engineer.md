E2B is hiring its first engineer responsible for Trust & Safety to own and develop the systems that protect its platform and customers from fraud and abuse. The work spans payment fraud, account abuse, and misuse of compute resources — performing investigations and building detections using automation to respond.

The role is hands-on: investigating third-party abuse reports, analyzing logs and querying data to connect activity across systems, building tools and automation for detection, investigation, and mitigation, and shaping trust and safety policies and response processes as the platform grows.

The role asks for experience in trust and safety, abuse prevention, or risk with a security focus, strong analytical skills, and the engineering ability to turn investigation findings into practical detections, tools, and automation. E2B uses Go, but prior Go experience is not required. The team works in person 4 days a week in its San Francisco and Prague offices.
