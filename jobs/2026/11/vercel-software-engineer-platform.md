Vercel is hiring a Software Engineer for its Platform team to build the observability systems that give Vercel visibility into how it builds, ships, and operates its systems. Core Platform owns Vercel's reliability, resilience, and engineering velocity, and the role covers observability for everything from cost attribution to deployment velocity, alongside the logs, metrics, and traces needed to optimize how Vercel builds its systems.

The work includes designing and building high-scale internal observability systems, building cost attribution and chargeback tooling, partnering with Backend and Platform teams to instrument services for compliance and auditing, and evaluating and operating observability infrastructure across existing vendors and the CNCF ecosystem.

The role asks for proficiency in TypeScript with backend and infrastructure experience, hands-on experience with Go, Rust, or Zig in production, experience with distributed computing systems at scale, and familiarity with observability tooling such as Datadog, Grafana, New Relic, Splunk, or CNCF-based stacks. The role can be onsite, hybrid, or remote; if based within commuting distance of an office (San Francisco, New York, London, or Berlin), it includes in-office anchor days on Monday, Tuesday, and Friday. The San Francisco base pay range for this role is $208,000–$312,000.
