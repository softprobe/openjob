Unkey is hiring a Site Reliability Engineer to build and operate Unkey's network and compute platform. The role helps engineers ship safely and keeps production reliable.

The role asks for 5+ years running production EKS, and the on-call rotation follows the sun. It is fully remote, working from anywhere in the world as long as you are available during Americas time zone hours.
