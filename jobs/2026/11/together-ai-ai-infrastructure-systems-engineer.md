Together AI is hiring an AI Infrastructure Systems Engineer to build and operate one of the world's largest GPU fleets used for frontier model training and inference. The role designs and builds fleet automation systems that provision, validate, deploy, upgrade, repair, and retire GPU clusters with minimal human intervention, and builds AI Infrastructure Agents that automate deployment, root-cause failures, incident triage, and autonomous remediation.

The role also builds Fleet Intelligence platforms that continuously monitor hardware health, firmware, networking, storage, thermals, and workload performance to predict failures before they impact customers. It is based in San Francisco.

The role asks for 3+ years building distributed systems, infrastructure platforms, or large-scale backend software, strong software engineering skills in Python, Go, or Rust, and experience with Linux, Kubernetes, Terraform, Ansible, or similar infrastructure technologies. The listed compensation band is $190,000–$270,000 plus equity and benefits.
