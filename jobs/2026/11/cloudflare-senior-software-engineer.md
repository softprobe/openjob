Cloudflare is hiring a Senior Software Engineer for its Developer Productivity team, which builds the internal tools and platforms that improve the velocity and developer experience of Cloudflare's engineering teams. The team owns developer productivity from code commit to production, developer insights and metrics, developer infrastructure including CI/CD and build systems, AI-assisted development, and Cloudflare-on-Cloudflare dogfooding.

This team is responsible for AI-assisted development across all of Cloudflare engineering, where adoption has grown 4–6x in the last year. As a Software Engineer, you will help build and operate internal platforms that help Cloudflare engineers develop, deploy, and run software safely and efficiently — working across developer tooling, CI/CD, GitOps, and AI-assisted development, including MCP servers, agents, evals, and context engineering. You will also help govern and enforce Cloudflare's Engineering Codex by turning engineering standards into automated guardrails, paved paths, and actionable feedback.

The role asks for experience with TypeScript, Go, or Bash, strong debugging and source-control skills, and experience with CI/CD, GitOps, or developer platforms. Bonus points for experience with AI agents, MCP, evals, or LLM-based tooling, policy-as-code or compliance automation, and operating distributed or Kubernetes-based systems. Locations are Austin, New York City, London, and Lisbon. The role was posted November 17, 2026.
