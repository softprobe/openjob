Arize AI is hiring a Senior AI Product Manager to own Evals & Annotations end to end — the evaluation lifecycle from eval creation and execution to human review, annotation workflows, and iteration — at the AI and agent engineering observability and evaluation platform.

The role sets the product strategy and roadmap, drives a best-in-class evaluation experience, prototypes directly with Design and Engineering, and spends significant time with customers each week. It asks for significant product management experience owning technically complex developer-facing products, a strong understanding of LLM evaluations and AI development workflows, and hands-on engineering ability (debugging, small fixes or PRs, prototypes). The listed salary is $200,000–$250,000 plus equity.

Arize is a remote-first company, with offices in New York City and the San Francisco Bay Area for those who wish to work in person.
