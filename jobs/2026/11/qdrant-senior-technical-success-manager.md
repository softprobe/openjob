Qdrant is hiring its first Senior Technical Success Manager in Australia or Japan, reporting to the Director of Customer Success. The role owns the post-sale relationship for Qdrant's largest customers in the region, from onboarding through production, scaling, and expansion, and is the technical face of Qdrant in APAC.

Most of the time goes to helping customers who already run Qdrant in production succeed and grow; the rest goes to new deals, partner work, and first-line technical questions in APAC hours, across Qdrant Cloud and self-hosted Kubernetes environments.

The role asks for 6+ years in customer-facing technical roles at an AI, data, or database infrastructure company, hands-on cloud-native infrastructure (Kubernetes, Terraform, and at least one major cloud), and being based in APAC, ideally Australia.
