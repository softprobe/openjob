Databricks is hiring a Staff Software Engineer - Fullstack for its newest R&D center in Vancouver, expanding its presence in the Pacific Northwest. As a fullstack engineer, you will work with your team and product management to make insights from data simple, setting the foundation for how Databricks builds robust, scalable, and delightful products.

Vancouver teams work across several strategic areas, including Log Analytics — analyzing petabyte-scale logs in real time — AI/BI, which is redefining business intelligence for the AI age, Unity Catalog Business Semantics, and Databricks Apps, one of the company's fastest-growing products.

The role asks for 10+ years of experience with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, a passion for user experience and front-end architecture, 10+ years of experience with server-side web technologies such as Node.js, Java, Python, Scala, C#, C++, or Go, good knowledge of SQL, and experience with cloud technologies and large-scale distributed systems. The Canada pay range for this role is $173,400–$238,350 CAD.
