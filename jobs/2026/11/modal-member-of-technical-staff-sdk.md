Modal is hiring a Member of Technical Staff for its SDK team — strong engineers with experience building software that developers love to work with, and a demonstrated drive to build beautiful abstractions that enhance developer productivity.

The role improves the developer and agent experience of Modal's SDK and CLI and develops new primitives for entirely new areas that arise as the industry transforms, working directly with users.

The role asks for 5+ years of experience developing high-quality Python, JavaScript, or Go applications or libraries used by other developers, a strong code-as-product sense focused on developer ergonomics, and an understanding of async programming and concurrency. Experience with developer tools or SDKs, maintaining open-source software, and agentic development workflows is a plus. The role works in person in the New York office and joins the on-call rotation.
