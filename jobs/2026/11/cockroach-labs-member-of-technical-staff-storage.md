Cockroach Labs is hiring a Staff Software Engineer to join its Storage team to help shape and build CockroachDB's storage systems. Storage is the foundation of CockroachDB, responsible for the safe, durable, and efficient persistence and retrieval of data, on top of which the rest of the database is built.

The team develops and maintains Pebble, a log-structured merge-tree storage engine purpose-built for multi-version concurrency control, and Plenum, the elastic, disaggregated storage layer that separates a cluster's storage from its compute and allows them to scale independently. In this role, you will bring deep storage expertise to complex problems spanning Pebble, Plenum, encryption at rest, CockroachDB's storage interfaces, and the cloud environments in which they operate — working hands-on, leading the design and delivery of significant improvements, and helping the team make sound tradeoffs among durability, performance, scalability, cost, and operational simplicity.

The role asks for 8+ years building complex distributed systems or storage software, storage architecture expertise such as log-structured merge-trees or cloud object storage, production experience with large-scale distributed systems, and production-ready code in systems programming languages like Go, Rust, or C/C++. Locations are New York, NY; Remote, US; San Mateo, CA; and Toronto, Canada, with a hybrid model for those local to an office. The annual anticipated base salary range for this role is $180,000–$238,100 USD.
