Softprobe is hiring a software engineer. The company makes performance reviews for AI agents: see where an agent underperforms, understand why, and prove the next version is better. The public site is https://www.softprobe.ai.

The work is the product itself. That includes session capture and ingestion, the session and step explorer, Agent Diagnosis, and integrations with agent frameworks such as OpenCode. A captured session can be reviewed step by step: the exact input, output, timing, and failures. Agent Diagnosis is coming soon. It names the performance problem with evidence and a concrete next step.

The role is based in Los Altos, California or Shanghai. Remote work is fine.

Apply at https://www.linkedin.com/company/softprobe/jobs/.
