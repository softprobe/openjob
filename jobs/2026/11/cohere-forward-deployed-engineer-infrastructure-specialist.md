Cohere is hiring a Forward Deployed Engineer, Infrastructure Specialist to partner with Canadian public sector organisations, unlocking value through secure, ethical deployment of generative AI. The role works directly with public sector customers to understand their greatest problems and design and implement solutions using Cohere's stack, as a bridge between the North product and clients' engineering teams.

The role leads end-to-end deployment of North in private cloud and on-premises environments, including planning, configuration, testing, and rollout, and partners with enterprise IT teams on infrastructure, security requirements, and data management. It is based in Ottawa, with 20–40% travel anticipated. Active Top Secret clearance is strongly preferred; candidates eligible and willing to obtain clearance will also be considered.

The role asks for experience deploying enterprise software in private or hybrid cloud environments, administering production Kubernetes clusters with Helm, and strong expertise in cloud infrastructure (Azure, AWS, GCP), networking, and virtualization.
