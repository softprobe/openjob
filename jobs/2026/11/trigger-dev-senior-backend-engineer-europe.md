Trigger.dev is hiring a Senior Backend Engineer based in Europe to help build its Open Source and Cloud product — the platform for running reliable AI agents and workflows.

The role designs and implements SDK and platform features end to end, develops database schemas and data models, creates and optimises query performance and data access patterns, and builds backend systems and scalable APIs that stand up to production load, with security as a default and observability tooling throughout.

The role asks for deep expertise in Node.js and TypeScript, Postgres and Redis, and cloud infrastructure on AWS, GCP, or Azure, plus comfort being on call. A proven track record of contributing to open source, experience with high-throughput systems, and familiarity with Go, Kubernetes, or OpenTelemetry are pluses. The team works async, with in-person events through the year, and offers 25 days of vacation plus equity.
