Pinecone is hiring a Staff Developer Advocate to own the Pinecone community — online and in person — with a particular focus on building a strong San Francisco / Bay Area presence.

The role speaks at AI and developer conferences and Bay Area meetups and owns Pinecone's talk pipeline, evangelizes Pinecone to Bay Area engineering teams through technical sessions and workshops, builds working demos and turns each talk into a blog post and video, and brings what developers need back to Product and Engineering.

The role asks for 7+ years in technical roles, including 3+ years in developer advocacy or a similar public-facing technical role, a track record of speaking at developer conferences and meetups, a portfolio of demos, sample apps, technical blog posts, and videos, and strong hands-on coding skills in Python and at least one other language (TypeScript preferred).
