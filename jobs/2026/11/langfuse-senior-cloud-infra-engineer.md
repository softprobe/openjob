Langfuse is hiring a Senior Cloud Infra Engineer to keep Langfuse running everywhere. Langfuse processes over a billion trace events per month, and the role owns uptime, performance, and cost efficiency across the entire cloud footprint, and makes sure every self-hosted deployment runs just as smoothly.

The role operates Langfuse Cloud on AWS ECS Fargate and ClickHouse Cloud, owns the public self-hosted infrastructure including the Helm chart and Docker Compose setup, builds world-class observability on Datadog, and automates everything — CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure-as-code, automated scaling, and zero-downtime deployments. It is fully remote, in a globally distributed team.

The role asks for a strong infrastructure or SRE engineer with experience operating production workloads on AWS, comfort with container orchestration (Kubernetes and/or ECS, Helm, Docker), experience with infrastructure-as-code, and strong monitoring and observability instincts.
