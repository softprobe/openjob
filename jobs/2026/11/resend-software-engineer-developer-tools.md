Resend is hiring a Software Engineer, Developer Tools for its DevTools Squad. The role works across the SDKs, React Email, CLI, MCP, and integrations, alongside the backend services that support them, helping developers build within their existing workflows, understand failures, and depend on Resend as their applications grow.

The work includes building and maintaining developer tools that make Resend easy to integrate with across languages, frameworks, and environments, building the interfaces developers and agents use to work with Resend — such as MCP, skills, and the CLI — and taking care of compatibility, testing, releases, and upgrades. It also means working directly with developers through support, GitHub issues, and pull requests.

The role asks for strong backend engineering experience building and maintaining APIs or services in production, experience in TypeScript and Node.js, and an understanding of authentication, authorization, retries, idempotency, rate limits, and partial failures. It is fully remote, based in Americas or European time zones, with some travel for team offsites, conferences, and meetups. The listed compensation is $130,000–$160,000 USD, commensurate with location and experience.
