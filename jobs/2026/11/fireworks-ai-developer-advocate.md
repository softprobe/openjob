Fireworks AI is hiring a Developer Advocate — one of the first advocate seats on the team, at Senior or Staff level — to make Fireworks the platform developers reach for first when they want to build, fine-tune, and serve frontier open models in production.

The role ships cookbooks, quickstarts, reference applications, and end-to-end tutorials; owns the launch response when a major model lands; speaks at conferences, hackathons, and meetups; and brings developer friction and feature gaps back to Product and Engineering with evidence. It is based in San Mateo, CA or London, with roughly 20–25% travel in a typical quarter.

The role asks for 5+ years of combined engineering and developer-facing experience, strong Python, hands-on work with LLM APIs in production, at least one agent framework, and personal experience fine-tuning, evaluating, and deploying a model — plus a public track record of technical writing, talks, videos, or open-source work.
