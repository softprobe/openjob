Stripe is hiring a Staff Software Engineer for Code Forge, the team in Developer Productivity responsible for the systems that store Stripe's code and its history and serve it to every engineer, AI agent, and CI job. The team builds source control that scales to meet any demand and serves each of its users in the way that best fits their work — engineers who write and review code, AI agents that work in parallel, and CI jobs that build and test every change.

In this role, you will lead how Stripe stores and serves its code as demand grows: setting technical direction, leading large projects across engineers and teams, and writing a large part of the code yourself. You will design and build storage systems for code and its history that scale with Stripe's massive monorepo and the growing load from engineers, agents, and CI, and partner with the Developer AI and Developer Environments teams to remove latency in the paths engineers, agents, and CI use most.

The role requires 10+ years of professional software engineering experience, including designing, building, and operating distributed systems, databases, or storage infrastructure in production, experience leading large, complex projects across teams, and experience using AI coding agents in daily work. The role is available either in an office or a remote location (35+ miles from a Stripe office). The annual US base salary range for this role is $224,000–$336,000.
