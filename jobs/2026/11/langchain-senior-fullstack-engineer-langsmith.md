LangChain is hiring a Senior Fullstack Engineer for the LangSmith team, which owns and builds LangChain's core platform for observability, evaluation, and production reliability of AI systems — from tracing and annotation to run rules, evaluations, and beyond.

The role shapes the technical direction of the platform across a Go, Python, and TypeScript stack, working closely with enterprise customers and developer end-users. It is onsite 5 days a week in Boston, MA.

The role asks for 5+ years of software engineering on complex platform products, fullstack experience with Go or Python on the backend and React + TypeScript on the frontend, and a strong understanding of database systems, particularly Postgres and Redis. Experience with AI/LLM-powered applications, developer tools, or observability platforms is a bonus. The listed compensation band is $175,000–$240,000.
