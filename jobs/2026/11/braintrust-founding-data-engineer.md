Braintrust is hiring a Founding Data Engineer to establish its data foundation as the company grows quickly across enterprise and self-serve customers. It is a founding, hands-on role: deciding what good looks like, building it, and working directly with the executive team to shape the data function as it grows.

The core of the job is analytics engineering — defining the business entities and metrics the company runs on, modeling them so they can be trusted, and making them accessible without a data person in the loop. Around that core, the role owns the ingestion pipelines that feed it, from product events and CRM to vendor APIs and webhooks, and stands up self-serve analytics on top of it.

The role asks for a strong command of SQL and dbt, experience with a cloud warehouse such as Snowflake and an orchestrator such as Dagster or Airflow, and enough engineering depth to own pipelines end to end in production code. It is based in San Francisco.
