Convex is hiring a Software Engineer, AI to make Convex the best platform for building agentic apps and agents. The work ranges from small ergonomic tweaks to make agentic development seamless, to large structural changes like branching and automatic performance optimization of Convex projects, and keeping on top of and ahead of technological shifts.

The role is a high-autonomy role on a small, high-leverage team: working across Convex's APIs, plugins, and AI framework integrations, staying close to how developers actually build with Convex, and strengthening the case that Convex is the best platform for agentic development through demos, examples, and working directly with customers. The team works in-person in San Francisco, with some experienced folks remote.

The role asks for 4+ years of experience shipping and owning developer-facing products, APIs, or tooling, strong instincts for what makes a developer tool feel right, genuine curiosity about how AI is changing the way software gets built, and high-quality code (TypeScript experience a plus).
