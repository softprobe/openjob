Supabase is hiring a Software Engineer for Auth, one of the most popular products in the Supabase stack. Auth is written in Go on the server, with client libraries for TypeScript, SSR, and other frameworks, and the role covers building new Auth features and maintaining existing ones.

The work includes designing and implementing secure, scalable authentication features in Go and TypeScript, owning the performance, reliability, and scalability of the Auth server across Supabase's infrastructure, and evolving the Auth architecture — including support for OAuth, OIDC, SAML, and other protocols. It also means planning safe database migrations across a large fleet of Postgres instances and building observability — metrics, tracing, alerting, and dashboards — to keep the system healthy at scale.

The role requires 4+ years of professional experience writing and shipping Go in production, 2+ years working on an authentication system, and strong relational database experience. The team is fully remote and works asynchronously, with no offices — Supabase hires globally.
