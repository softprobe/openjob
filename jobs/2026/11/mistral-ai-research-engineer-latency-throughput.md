Mistral AI is hiring a Research Engineer for its Latency & Throughput team, pushing the frontier of LLM inference by researching and prototyping techniques that reduce latency and increase throughput — from speculative decoding and quantization to KV compression and new attention variants.

The role sits between Mistral's inference engineers, who own the production serving stack, and its research scientists. It is science-focused: generating new ideas, keeping up with the state of the art, and implementing proof-of-concepts that bridge academic results and production impact. Success is measured by demonstrated latency or throughput gains, first at small scale, then adopted in production.

The role asks for a strong ML background with solid knowledge of LLM training techniques, hands-on Python and PyTorch, and research experience of some form — a thesis, publications, a dedicated research project, or efficiency-oriented work in a lab or industry setting. A PhD is not required. The role is based in the Paris or London offices (hybrid).
