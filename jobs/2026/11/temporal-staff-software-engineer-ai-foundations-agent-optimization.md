Temporal is hiring a Staff Software Engineer, AI Foundations (Agent Optimization). Temporal provides a reliable foundation powering AI leaders such as OpenAI, NVIDIA, Cursor, and Lovable, and the mission of the AI Foundations team is to accelerate Temporal adoption across the entire ecosystem.

In this role, you will lead Temporal's agent optimization efforts: designing tools and mechanisms to help Temporal users build agents optimized for token spend and response time while maintaining result quality — model routing is a first step, with multi-agent architecture, cache policy, and context management all relevant. You will work closely with AI Foundations team members who focus on agentic development, maintain agent skills that lift the performance of Codex, Claude Code, and similar tools, and build ecosystem integrations and policy and security systems.

The role asks for 8+ years of professional work experience, proven experience optimizing AI systems using both machine learning and systems techniques, fluency in multiple programming languages (Python, TypeScript, Java, Go), and deep experience with concurrent programming. Temporal is remote-first. No salary is stated in the posting. The role was published November 20, 2026.
