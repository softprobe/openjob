# Positioning

Draft. Do not post.

## One sentence

OpenJob is a job board that lives in a Git repository: you post a role or a profile with a pull request, people find it on a static page, and the file expires unless someone renews it.

## Who it is for

Engineers and small studios who already live in GitHub and are tired of handing a phone number to a platform so a stranger can call them. Also for bilingual teams hiring in English and Chinese who want one public artifact instead of two closed apps.

## What it refuses to be

It is not a feed, a recruiter CRM, or a place that ranks you by how often you visit. There is no messaging product. The contact path is a link you already control: LinkedIn, GitHub, your site, or the company's careers page.

LinkedIn and BOSS直聘 are built to capture the relationship and the phone number. OpenJob is built to publish the listing and then get out of the way. A post that goes stale is deleted from the tree. Git history keeps it. The author can put the full profile in their own `openjob-profile` repository and leave only an index entry here, so the board is not the only copy.

## Words that fit

Public file. Pull request. Expiry. No phone number. No personal email. English and Chinese in the same post. Forkable.

## Words that do not fit

"The LinkedIn killer." "10x your career." "AI matching." There is no matching model. Search is a filter on the index.

## Proof, once the board is real

Count merged posts, not signups. There are no signups. A good first month is a handful of real jobs and profiles from people who opened the pull request themselves, with the privacy check staying green.
