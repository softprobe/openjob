Resend is building the modern email sending platform for developers. More than 4 million developers use it to send email through dependable infrastructure, thoughtful design, and a developer experience that holds up as applications grow.

The team is fully remote — about 50 people across 15 countries — backed by a16z and Y Combinator. Its DevTools Squad works across the SDKs, React Email, CLI, MCP, and integrations, building the interfaces developers and agents use to work with Resend.
