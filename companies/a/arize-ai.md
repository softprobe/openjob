Arize AI is an AI and agent engineering observability and evaluation platform. It unifies build, test, and run in a single workspace so AI engineers can ship high-performing, reliable agents and applications, from first prototype to production scale.

Arize serves more than 150 leading enterprises and Fortune 500 companies, including Booking.com, Uber, Siemens, and PepsiCo. The company raised a $70M Series C in February 2025, and its job posting notes that Arize has been acquired while continuing to operate with the urgency of an early-stage startup.
