Asymptote Labs is building the AI Detection & Response layer for the agentic enterprise. AI agents are becoming enterprise infrastructure, but security teams have no unified system of record for what they're doing. Asymptote delivers the telemetry, detection, and response layer that makes AI agent activity observable and secure.

Beacon is its open-source foundation, named one of the hottest open-source cybersecurity tools in June 2026. The company raised a $5.5M seed round in October 2026 to build the visibility and control plane for AI agents.
