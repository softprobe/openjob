Anyscale commercializes Ray, the open-source distributed computing framework, and builds the platform to run Ray so teams can scale ML applications from a laptop to the cluster. Ray is in production at companies including OpenAI, Uber, Spotify, Instacart, and Cruise.

Anyscale is backed by Andreessen Horowitz, NEA, and Addition, with $250M+ raised to date. NScale agreed to acquire Anyscale in July 2026.
