Airbyte is on a mission to make data available and actionable to everyone, everywhere. With 700+ connectors, its platform powers analytics and gives AI agents the business context they need to do real work. More than 25,000 companies, including 20% of the Fortune 500, rely on Airbyte.

Airbyte is built on open source, with a community of 27,000 members, and is backed by $181M from investors including Benchmark, Accel, Altimeter, Coatue, and Y Combinator.
