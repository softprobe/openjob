Browserbase is the complete platform for building and deploying AI agents that browse and interact with the web like humans. With a single API key, developers can enable agents to search and retrieve information, fill out forms, and take action across websites.

Browserbase powers more than 50 million browser sessions each month for over 10,000 customers, including Ramp, DeepMind, Clay, Vercel, and Notion. It is a Series B startup backed by Kleiner Perkins, CRV, and Notable Capital, and its team builds in the open with the open-source community.
