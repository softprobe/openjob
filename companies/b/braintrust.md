Braintrust is the agent observability platform. By applying intelligence to agent traces and automatically surfacing the most critical patterns, it gives teams the visibility to understand how agents behave in production and the tools to improve them.

Teams at Notion, Stripe, Box, OpenAI, and Cloudflare use Braintrust to trace their agents, find the issues in their observability data, and run evals that tell them how to improve.
