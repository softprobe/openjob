E2B is building the Agent Cloud — the infrastructure behind AI labs and some of the most widely used consumer and enterprise agents, including Manus, Genspark, Lindy, Groq, and Artificial Analysis. The company is a fast-growing Series A startup with over $37M in funding and 9-figure revenue.

The team includes the original authors of Firecracker, the AWS microVM technology powering Lambda, along with alumni of Cognition, JetBrains, Zapier, and Wish. E2B has offices in San Francisco and Prague.
