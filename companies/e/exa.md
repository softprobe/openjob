Exa is an applied AI lab building a search engine unlike the world has ever seen. It builds massive-scale infrastructure to crawl the entire web, trains state-of-the-art embedding models to process it, and designs high-performance vector databases to retrieve over it.

Exa now powers search for Cursor, Cognition, HubSpot, and over 400,000 developers, and has raised $350M from Lightspeed, Benchmark, and a16z. Its goal is to build perfect search over all the world's information.
