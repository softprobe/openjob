Upstash is building the next generation data platform for the serverless and edge era. Its cloud platform lets users deploy data services in seconds — Serverless Redis, Vector, Search, QStash, Workflow, Box and Blob — and start using them immediately, paying per request with a free tier.

At the infrastructure level, the team runs production on Kubernetes, Golang, and Terraform across AWS and GCP. The company is remote-first, with the team working from anywhere in the world.
