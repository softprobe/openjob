Convex builds the backend platform for building agentic applications and agents. Its team has built and designed some of the largest platforms in the world, and works in-person together in its San Francisco office, hiring primarily in SF with a few experienced folks working remotely.

The company believes it is not sufficient to be the best platform for agentic development — it also has to be the best platform for building agentic applications, while maintaining architectural principles that keep applications simple, correct, and secure over time.
