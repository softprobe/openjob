Chroma builds the data and search infrastructure for AI: an open-source embedding database that makes it easy to build AI applications with embeddings. Its distributed data plane supports vector search, document storage, and metadata filtering at scale.

Chroma was founded in 2022 in San Francisco. Its acquisition by Replit was announced in October 2026.
