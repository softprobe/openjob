For more than a decade, Cockroach Labs has built database technology trusted to power some of the world's most mission-critical applications. Cockroach Continuum brings individual databases together into one fleet designed for agentic applications, where AI agents help teams provision, scale, and optimize workloads at machine speed.

The Storage team develops and maintains Pebble, a log-structured merge-tree storage engine purpose-built for multi-version concurrency control, and Plenum, the elastic, disaggregated storage layer that lets a cluster's storage and compute scale independently.
