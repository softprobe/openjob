PostHog is your product's context layer. It ingests and stores analytics, errors, replays, and business data so you and your agents can query and act on it. More than 500,000 organizations have installed PostHog, mostly driven by word-of-mouth.

PostHog launched out of Y Combinator's W20 cohort and has raised more than $180M. The company is natively remote and fully open-source, with small autonomous teams, transparent pay published through a public salary calculator, and revenue over $50M a year.
