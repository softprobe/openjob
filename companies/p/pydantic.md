Pydantic builds the data validation library that powers modern Python development, with 500 million downloads per month. The same team builds Pydantic AI, the agent framework, and Logfire, the observability platform with first-class support for AI engineering.

The company commits over 20% of its engineering team to maintaining and expanding its open-source ecosystem, including the core Pydantic Validation library and Pydantic AI. It is backed by Sequoia Capital and runs a fully remote team across multiple time zones, with regular in-person offsites.
