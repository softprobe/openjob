Grafana Labs is the company behind Grafana Cloud, the fully managed observability platform trusted by more than 10,000 organizations to ensure reliability, resolve incidents faster, and optimize telemetry at scale. Built on open source and open standards, Grafana Cloud brings AI to observability and observability to AI, giving teams — and their agents — unified visibility across their data.

Grafana Labs is a 100% remote company with team members across 40+ countries. Customers include Anthropic, Bloomberg, NVIDIA, Microsoft, and Salesforce. The company is now building an AI-native data intelligence system that gives agents reliable, governed access to enterprise context.
