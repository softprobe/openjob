Groq is an American AI company that builds an AI accelerator ASIC — its Language Processing Unit (LPU) — designed for AI model inference, delivering ultra-low latency performance and record-breaking inference results for real-time AI applications.

The company is headquartered in Mountain View, California, with 30 open positions on its job board and hiring hubs in the Dallas-Fort Worth Area, the San Francisco Bay Area, and the New York City Area. In December 2025 Groq signed a non-exclusive licensing agreement with Nvidia while remaining independent.
