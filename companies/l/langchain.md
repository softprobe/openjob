LangChain builds the open-source frameworks and the LangSmith platform for building, evaluating, deploying, and operating AI agents. Its mission is to enable every company to own its intelligence: the LangSmith platform gives developers and enterprises the infrastructure to run, improve, and govern agents at scale.

More than 7,000 organizations rely on LangSmith, from fast-growing startups to Fortune 500 enterprises, including NVIDIA, Bridgewater, LinkedIn, Workday, and Rippling. The company has raised $460M to date, with its most recent funding round valuing the company at $3B.
