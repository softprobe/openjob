LlamaIndex is an AI company building document understanding and data infrastructure for AI applications. Its open-source LlamaIndex framework connects custom data sources to large language models for retrieval-augmented generation and agents.

The document understanding team works on vision-language models, document processing, data curation, synthetic data generation, benchmarking, training, fine-tuning, and post-training, turning research prototypes into production systems used by customers.
