Langfuse builds observability tooling for LLM applications, processing over a billion trace events per month across its cloud and self-hosted deployments. Its cloud runs on AWS ECS Fargate and ClickHouse Cloud, and its public self-hosted infrastructure includes a Helm chart and Docker Compose setup, so teams from startups to enterprises can run Langfuse on their own terms.

Langfuse is now part of ClickHouse, and the company is globally distributed and remote-friendly, operating in over 25 countries.
