Zed is building tools for people who care deeply about making software. The team builds the Zed editor and the cloud services behind Zed and Delta, with a server-side infrastructure that powers AI connections, collaboration primitives, and cross-cutting concerns like auth, billing, and telemetry.

The cloud backend is built in Rust, including services compiled to WebAssembly and running on Cloudflare Workers. The team is remote, working in the Eastern Time Zone.
