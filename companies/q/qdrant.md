Qdrant is an open-source vector search engine powering AI applications, from semantic search and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to AI agents and real-time recommendations. It is building the retrieval infrastructure layer for modern AI.

Qdrant is trusted by Canva, HubSpot, Tripadvisor, Bosch, and Deutsche Telekom, and recently raised $50M in Series B funding. The company is remote-first.
