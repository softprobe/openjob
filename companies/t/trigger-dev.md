Trigger.dev is the platform for running reliable AI agents and workflows. Developers use it to build, deploy, and scale production systems with built-in tools for orchestration, scheduling, monitoring, and debugging.

Its Cloud product is a managed service that deploys users' code and auto-scales from zero to millions of executions. Today it serves thousands of teams building AI apps and agents, handling hundreds of millions of executions per month. Trigger.dev is a commercial open-source company with a small, flat team that works async and remote.
