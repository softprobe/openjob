Speakeasy provides the AI control plane for enterprises. Its platform helps companies securely scale usage of AI, with a way to centrally manage MCPs, Skills, and Assistants across the whole company, with fine-grained permissions, threat detection, and full observability.

Speakeasy is a fast-growing, venture-backed team based in San Francisco and London, with a track record of building developer tools and infrastructure used from Fortune 500 companies to well-known startups.
