Sentry helps developers fix errors and performance issues before users notice, so teams can spend less time firefighting and more time building. Trusted by 200,000+ organizations, Sentry is today's application monitoring standard, and the team is building its AI-native future.

Its Platform and Infrastructure Engineering teams build and operate the systems that power Sentry — cloud infrastructure, distributed storage, and real-time event processing — providing the technical foundation that enables the products and engineering organization to grow.
