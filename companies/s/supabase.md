Supabase is the Postgres development platform, built by developers for developers. It provides a complete backend solution including Database, Auth, Storage, Edge Functions, Realtime, and Vector Search, all deeply integrated and designed for growth.

The team is born-remote and open-source-first — about 400 people in 60+ countries, with a community of 540,000+ members. Supabase hires globally and believes you can do your best work from anywhere.
