Inngest builds novel infrastructure for AI-first companies — event-driven workflows and queueing that developers use to build reliable systems. Its backend runs on Go, Postgres, Redis, PubSub, and FoundationDB, with a TypeScript, React, and Next.js frontend.

The company is a hybrid San Francisco team that dogfoods its own product, works in public with an active community on Discord and GitHub, and treats helping developers succeed as mission critical.
