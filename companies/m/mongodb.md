MongoDB builds the unified data platform for the AI era, led by MongoDB Atlas, its globally distributed, multi-cloud database. More than 67,000 customers, including 75% of the Fortune 100, rely on MongoDB for their most important applications.

The Storage Layer Services team is re-architecting the cloud storage layer at the heart of Atlas, building performant, multi-tenant distributed storage services that enhance today's Atlas storage stack and enable more customer workloads to run efficiently.
