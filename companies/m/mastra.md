Mastra is the open-source TypeScript framework for building AI agents. Built by the team behind Gatsby and backed by Y Combinator and Spark Capital, with $35M raised total, it went from 0 to 30k+ GitHub stars and 3M+ weekly npm downloads in less than 2 years.

The team is small, shipping fast, and fully remote, working mostly across North American and European time zones.
