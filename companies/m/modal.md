Modal is building the infrastructure layer for AI — instant GPU access, sub-second container starts, and native storage, so it is simple to serve low-latency inference, fine-tune models, and access production-ready sandboxes at scale. Customers include Ramp, Cognition, DoorDash, and Suno.

The company has passed $1B in annualized run rate, up 15x from a year ago, and raised a $750M Series D in October 2026 led by Accel at a $15.75B valuation. The team includes creators of popular open-source projects, academic researchers, and international olympiad medalists.
